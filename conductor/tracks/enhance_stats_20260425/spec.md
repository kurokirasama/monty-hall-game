# Specification: Enhance detailed statistics tracking and visualizations

## Overview
This track focuses on improving the statistics tracking of the Monty Hall Simulator. It aims to provide users with more detailed metrics and visual charts to better understand the probability outcomes of their choices (switching vs. staying).

## Functional Requirements
- Track and store historical game results (win/loss, switched/stayed).
- Display a visual chart or graph comparing the win rates of switching vs. staying.
- Ensure the UI updates in real-time as new games are played.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Visuals must adhere to the strict retro pixel-art aesthetic.
- UI must remain simple, direct, and responsive.

## Acceptance Criteria
- User can see a detailed breakdown of their statistics.
- A visual representation (e.g., bar chart) clearly shows the difference in win rates.
