# Product Guidelines

## Tone & Voice
- **Simple & Direct:** Keep text minimal and focused on action. Communicate instructions and outcomes clearly without unnecessary fluff, ensuring users can quickly understand their choices.

## Visual Aesthetic & Branding
- **DESIGN.md Reference:** All visual components, colors, and typography must adhere to the tokens and rationale defined in [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md).
- **Strict Retro:** Adhere strictly to the pixel-art aesthetic. Use 8-bit fonts, blocky graphics, and a distinct retro color palette for all UI elements to maintain a cohesive vintage game feel.

## UX Principles
- **Instant Feedback:** Every user interaction must have an immediate visual or audio cue (e.g., sound effects on win/loss, immediate UI updates when picking a door).
- **Clear Explanations:** Provide intuitive guides or tooltips that help users understand the underlying math and probability concepts of the Monty Hall problem without overwhelming them with text.
